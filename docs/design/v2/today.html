Today, rebuilt on the Flo skeleton

Flo's home puts one big circle in the middle that is the app's central act, small helpers beside it, and a short stack of context underneath. For Candy Corn the central act is talking, so the circle is Talk, with Write and Photo beside it. Everything under it is context you glance at, not read.

What earns a place on the front page

  1. Capture: Talk, Write, Photo. The reason the app exists. Biggest thing on screen, one tap from anywhere.
  2. How you are: the three numbers, compact, one tap to change. Not a chart.
  3. The next appointment: how soon, with whom, how many things you have pinned, and the Prep action. This is the payoff of capturing.
  4. Up next: the one goal for today, with who set it, and a count of the rest. Todoist and Speak do this in their small widgets: one item, a number, no list.
  5. The week strip: which days you captured something, so continuity is visible without a streak.

What moved off the front page

  1. Candy Corn noticed (patterns) lives in History and in the Prep brief, where it has evidence next to it.
  2. Bring up next time is one tap away via the appointment card, and the full goal history via See all.
  3. Recent entries are the Journal tab.

Rules applied

One dominant object and at most three supporting decisions in the first viewport. Full-width rows instead of clipped horizontal rails. No sentence longer than one line. Kernel glyph and the three voices kept. Tabular numerals. White canvas, warm wash only inside the hero.
