# Candy Corn

A private second brain for your mental-health journey.

Candy Corn helps a person remember what happens in treatment, capture what happens between appointments, organize their thoughts, keep goals, and walk into therapy, TMS, or psychiatry appointments prepared.

It is not an AI therapist, a crisis service, or a replacement for professional care.

## Principles

- Local first. The canonical copy of your history lives on your own device.
- Patient owned. Inspect, export, and delete everything. No account required.
- AI optional. Every core feature works with AI turned off.
- Core access free. Nobody loses their history because they cannot pay.
- Human care reinforced, not replaced. The loop is capture, organize, remember, bring it back to a human.

## The loop

```
life  ->  journal / rant / mood  ->  care vault  ->  appointment prep  ->  therapy / TMS  ->  recording / homework / goals  ->  care vault  ->  life
```
